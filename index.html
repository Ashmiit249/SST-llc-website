About us

A Dubai-based tyre trading company

Sanjit Suri Trading LLC supplies truck and van tyres to fleet operators, transport companies, workshops and resellers. We carry both premium and budget ranges from the world’s leading manufacturers, sourced directly so our customers get genuine products at competitive prices.

From our base on Al Maktoum Hospital Road in Deira, we serve businesses across Dubai and the UAE, and welcome enquiries from the wider region.
